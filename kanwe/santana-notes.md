https://www.facebook.com/izmael.zantanacaztillo/ (display name on Facebook: "Santana Drogba").

Originals are not committed. Everything was re-encoded: photos → WebP at responsive sizes, clips → muted MP4 (H.264) + WebM (VP9) with WebP posters.
