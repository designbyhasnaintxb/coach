Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the training inquiry form:

  • who the training is for (a player, a parent or guardian asking for a player, a team or group, or someone else)
  • the training you're interested in, the player's experience level, and the player-path stage you chose (if you used it)
  • your name and email address, plus your phone number and any message you write (if you add them)

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your inquiry about soccer training with Coach Santana. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Coach Santana.

Saved on your device

If you choose a training direction on the site, that choice is remembered in your own browser (local storage) so the inquiry form can be pre-filled. It never leaves your device unless you send the form.

Fonts, scripts and video

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files. Training videos are served from this website itself.

Photos and videos of young players

Photos and clips on this site come from Coach Santana's own public posts. If you'd like an image of your child removed, mention it through the inquiry form and it will be taken down.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a note through the training inquiry form and mention it in your message.
