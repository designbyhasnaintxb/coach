Privacy

What this site collects

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send through the session request form:

  • the session you're asking about (60 minutes, 90 minutes, a group training inquiry, or "not sure")
  • who is getting in touch (the player, a parent or guardian, or someone else)
  • the player's name, plus their age, current experience and main goal (if you add them)
  • your name and email address, plus your phone number and preferred day and time (if you add them)
  • your payment preference. No card or payment details are collected: online payment is not connected.

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your session request and arrange training with Coach Kwame. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are received and stored by the website's hosting provider (Netlify) so they can be passed on to Coach Kwame.

Saved on your device

If you choose a session on the site, that choice is remembered in your own browser (local storage) so the request form can be pre-filled. It never leaves your device unless you send the form.

Fonts, scripts and video

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from public content-delivery networks, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files. Photos are served from this website itself.

Photos and videos of young players

Photos on this site were supplied by Coach Kwame. If you'd like an image of your child removed, mention it through the session request form and it will be taken down.

Changes or removal

If you'd like your details corrected or removed, send a note through the session request form and mention it in your message.
