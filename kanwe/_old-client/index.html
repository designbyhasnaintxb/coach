About Coach Santana

From player to coach.

Coach Santana played professional soccer in Mexico, went on to play in the United States, and has spent approximately ten years coaching in the U.S.

He also brings coaching experience from Mexico. Today he works with several groups at once — beginners finding their feet, young players building their game, and more advanced players and teams who want to keep developing.

His program is growing, especially with children and youth players. This site gives parents and players one place to understand his background and start a conversation.

Committed to the players in front of him — and to the community around the game.

What parents usually ask

Who is Coach Santana?

A soccer coach and former professional player in Mexico, who also played soccer in the United States.

What experience does he have?

Professional playing experience in Mexico, playing experience in the U.S., approximately ten years of coaching in the U.S. and additional coaching experience in Mexico.

Who does he train?

Beginners, youth players, and more advanced players and teams. Youth players are an important — and growing — part of his program.

What kind of training can I ask about?

Private, small-group, beginner development and advanced / team training are all directions you can ask about. Exact formats, schedules, locations and pricing are confirmed directly with Coach Santana.

What happens when I send an inquiry?

Your answers go straight to Coach Santana so he can reply about the options. Nothing is booked, reserved or paid through this site.
