About

Meet Coach Kwame.

Coach Kwame is an independent soccer coach focused on one thing: one-on-one player development.

Every session is personal — one player, one coach and one clear focus. The time is spent on that player's game, at that player's pace.

Group training is occasionally available too. If that's what you're looking for, just ask.

      How much does a session cost?

      A 60-minute one-on-one session is $35. A 90-minute one-on-one session is $55.

      What is a 1-on-1 session?

      Private training for one player with Coach Kwame. The whole session is built around that player's development — tell Kwame the player's main goal when you book.

      60 or 90 minutes — which should I choose?

      Both are the same personal, one-on-one format. The 90-minute session simply gives more training time. Not sure? Choose "Not sure" in the request and talk it through with Kwame.

      Do you offer group training?

      Group sessions may be available occasionally. Choose "Group training inquiry" when you send a request and Kwame will let you know.

      How does booking work?

      Send a session request with a preferred day and time. Nothing is booked instantly — Coach Kwame confirms the date, time and next steps with you directly.

      Can I pay online?

      Not yet. Online payment is shown as a preview only and isn't connected, so nothing is charged through this site.

      Where do sessions take place?

      The location is arranged with Coach Kwame when he confirms your session.
